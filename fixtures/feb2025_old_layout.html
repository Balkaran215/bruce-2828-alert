Bruce Power Staff Direction

All site access roads to Bruce Power remain closed.

All Category A, B and C staff are directed to not report for their scheduled shift Tuesday evening. If road conditions improve, some staff may receive a request from their supervisor to report to work to allow for a shift change.

Employees are responsible for checking road conditions in their area, driving safely and according to conditions, and obeying all signage and closed roads. It is against the law to drive on or cross a closed road.
